**laclonism** /ləˈkləʊnɪzəm/ — **n.**

Intercalative blend of *Claude* and *laconism* (< Greek *Lakōnikós* Laconian, through Latin *Lacōnicus*, with reference to Laconia, the Spartan region whose inhabitants avoided lengthy speech, with intrusive *-l-*).

The answering of a question by Claude in exactly the words the question needed and no others. **No attested instances.** The entry is retained on the reasoning that a language wants a word for the thing at the far end of the scale, even where the scale has only ever been read from one direction.
