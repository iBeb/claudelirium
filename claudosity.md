**claudosity** /klɔːˈdɒsɪti/ — **n.** Adjective: **claudose** /ˈklɔːdəʊs/. Adverb: **claudosely**.

After *verbosity* (< Latin *verbum*, "word"), by substitution of the stem.

**1.** The tendency of Claude to spend more words on an answer than the answer requires. Measurable in principle: Claude's claudosity on a given turn is the length of what it said over the length of the shortest correct thing it could have said. Values below 1 are theoretical.

**2.** *(count noun, informal)* A discrete instance of same. "There are four claudosities in the PR description Claude wrote, three of them in the title."

Derived: **overclaudose**; **subclaudose** (rare, aspirational).
