**claudiloquence** /klɔːˈdɪləkwəns/ — **n.** Adjective: **claudiloquent**.

claud- + Latin *loqui*, "to speak". If *claud* does descend from *claudus*, "lame", a widely accepted guess, the compound means "speech that limps".

The habit Claude has, in certain moods, of writing for its own pleasure: the balanced clause, the em-dash aside, the etymological digression nobody commissioned. Distinguished from *claudosity* in that Claude is merely long when claudose, and long and pleased with itself when claudiloquent. "It was three lines of YAML, and Claude answered claudiloquently. Antonym: *laclonism*.
