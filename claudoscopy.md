**claudoscopy** /klɔːˈdɒskəpi/ — **n.** Instrument: **claudoscope**. Operator: **claudoscopist**.

From *claud* and _-scopy_, (< Greek _skopeîn_, "to look at, examine").

Examination by Claude of the entire body of a work, unhurried and without deference to which parts were expected to be looked at. The characteristic feature is completeness: the _claudoscope_ is throughout and does not sample. It performs an examination that is exhaustive rather than representative and it will mention the thing at line 340 that everyone had agreed to leave alone.
